How it works, with no phone calls

Every step that agencies normally do on calls happens here in writing. You get speed and proof. If a step works for you, it stays in writing. That is the test for every part below.

Step 1 · Find us

One link, everywhere

LinkedIn posts, Upwork proposals, referrals, and the MAS network all point to one thing: the probe menu. There is no "book a call" button anywhere. The offer is a task, not a meeting.

Step 2 · Order

Probe ordered by message

Tap WhatsApp or email. The probe and price are already in the message. Payment is a single invoice link (Payoneer). No negotiation thread, no proposal deck.

Step 3 · Intake

A form, not an interview

A short form collects what we need: business details, links, brand assets, and view-only access to your accounts, with step-by-step screenshots. Questions after that are single messages, answered in the thread.

Step 4 · Deliver

Proof lands in 48 to 72 hours

The probe arrives in the same thread you ordered in, written to explain itself: findings, the work, and what to do next. This delivery, not a pitch, is what sells the retainer.

Step 5 · Convert

We ask about the retainer

After delivery, a short message sequence recaps the result, credits your probe fee, and offers the matching retainer with a package already built. Silence gets polite, spaced nudges. Never a cold call.

Step 6 · Retain

The retainer runs in writing too

Daily 08:00 Brain briefs, drafts to approve by replying YES, monthly ROI report in writing. Renewal is the default. You cancel by message, so the work has to keep earning its place.

What happens after your probe

Four short messages, sent only until you reply. Each one leads with what the probe found, not with MAS.

Day 0 · With delivery"Here is your probe. The headline: [top finding]. If we ran this monthly, [specific gain]. Your fee is credited if you start within 14 days."
Day 2 · The proof recapOne screenshot or number from the probe, plus the exact retainer package it maps to, price included. One tap to say yes.
Day 5 · The cost of waiting"The leak in Fix #1 costs roughly [amount] a month. The retainer pays for itself if we close half of it."
Day 9 · The clean close"Your probe credit expires in 5 days. Want me to hold it, or shall I close the file?" A clear no is a fine answer. The door stays open.
